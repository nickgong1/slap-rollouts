Real-robot rollouts

We show extra real robot rollouts from SLAP with different environmental perturbations we defined in the paper. We further show that we can combine some perturbations together and SLAP can still successfully finish the task.

SLAP and π0.5

We show some visual comparisons between SLAP and π0.5. We find that SLAP is especially strong when robot gripper starts from a shifted position.

LIBERO-Plus rollouts

We show eight successful trials from libero-Plus, two from each suite, together covering all seven perturbation types.
